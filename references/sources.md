## Source Hierarchy

1. **Primary reference for fidelity**: Spanish original, *Teoria y practica del Metodo Estructural Dinamico*.
2. **Primary reference for Italian terminology**: Italian translation, *Teoria e pratica del Metodo Strutturale Dinamico*.
3. **Archive reference**: CSU Salvatore Puledda archive page listing both ESP and ITA versions.
4. **External official mirror/context**: Centro Mundial de Estudios Humanistas page for the Spanish version.

When the Spanish and Italian wording differ, prefer the Spanish version for conceptual fidelity and the Italian version for user-facing Italian wording.

## Canonical Documents

### Spanish Original

**Citation label**: Pompei, Jorge y colaboradores. *Teoria y practica del Metodo Estructural Dinamico*. CEHBA, ed. 2008/06.

**Role in skill**: Use as the conceptual reference for the method.

**Direct PDF**: https://www.csusalvatorepuledda.org/dokuwiki/lib/exe/fetch.php?media=archivio%3A200806-metodo_estructural_dinamico-jorgepompei_etal-esp.pdf

**Notes**:

- The PDF title page gives *TEORIA Y PRACTICA DEL METODO ESTRUCTURAL DINAMICO* and "Jorge Pompei y Colaboradores".
- The preliminary note frames the text as a theoretical and practical development of the Metodo Estructural Dinamico.
- The text identifies Buenos Aires, June 2008.

### Italian Translation

**Citation label**: Pompei, Jorge e collaboratori. *Teoria e pratica del Metodo Strutturale Dinamico*. Traduzione dall'originale spagnolo coordinata da Roberta Consilvio. CEHBA, ed. 2008/06.

**Role in skill**: Use for Italian terminology, prompt wording, and user-facing names for MSD steps.

**Direct PDF**: https://www.csusalvatorepuledda.org/dokuwiki/lib/exe/fetch.php?media=archivio%3A200806-teoria_e_pratica_del_msd-j.pompei-ita.pdf

**Notes**:

- The title page gives *TEORIA E PRATICA DEL METODO STRUTTURALE DINAMICO* and "Jorge Pompei e collaboratori".
- It states that the translation from the Spanish original was coordinated by Roberta Consilvio.
- The practical guide includes the sections used by this skill: Domanda, Analisi, Risposta, Rapporto Finale.

## Citation Practice

- Cite the Spanish PDF when explaining the original method.
- Cite the Italian PDF when using Italian labels such as Domanda, Oggetto-Interesse, Analisi, Sintesi, Risposta.
- Cite the CSU archive page when explaining why the two documents should be considered paired versions.
- Cite the CMEH page only as supporting official context, not as a replacement for the CSU PDF links.
